# Unit – II

## 1. The Shield of Achilles – W. H. Auden

🌸 **Modern Modernist Poetry**

### 📌 Meaning of the Title:

* **Shield** = Protective armor carried by a warrior 🛡️
* **Achilles** = Greatest Greek hero of the Trojan War ⚔️

➡️ Means: *The shield belonging to the legendary warrior Achilles.*

---

### 🎯 Symbolism:

* The shield symbolizes **human civilization** 🌍
* Achilles symbolizes **heroism and warfare** ⚔️
* The images on the shield symbolize **the cruelty of the modern world** 💔

---

### 🧠 Why the title fits perfectly:

The poem contrasts the glorious world expected in Greek mythology with the violent and empty reality of the modern age.

Example:

> Thetis expects scenes of beauty on the shield but sees suffering and destruction instead.

👉 The title focuses on the poem's central object and symbol.

---

### 🔗 Relevance to the Poem:

The shield becomes a mirror of society.

Through it, Auden criticizes war, violence, and loss of humanity.

👉 The title highlights the poem's **main symbol and message.**

---

➡️ Means: *A train traveling toward Pakistan during Partition.*

* Train symbolizes **Partition and migration** 🚆

* Trains carrying dead bodies symbolize **violence and tragedy** 💔

---

### 🧠 Why the title fits perfectly:

The novel is set during the Partition of India in 1947.

Example:

> Trains become powerful symbols of both migration and communal violence.

👉 The title highlights the most important image in the novel.

---

### 🔗 Relevance to the Story:

The arrival and departure of trains influence the major events of the narrative.

The train represents the suffering caused by Partition.

👉 The title reflects the novel's **historical background and central symbol.**

---

### 🔗 Relevance to the Story:

The novel condemns prejudice and injustice against innocent people.

The mockingbird becomes the central symbol of innocence.

👉 The title highlights the novel's **main theme and message.**

## 12. A Thousand Splendid Suns – Khaled Hosseini

📖 **Contemporary Novel**

### 📌 Meaning of the Title:

* **Thousand** = Very many 🔢
* **Splendid** = Magnificent and beautiful ✨
* **Suns** = Sources of light and hope ☀️

➡️ Means: *Countless sources of beauty, hope, and brilliance.*

---

### 🎯 Symbolism:

* Suns symbolize **hope and resilience** ☀️
* Light symbolizes **strength amid suffering** ✨
* Kabul symbolizes **enduring beauty despite hardship** 🏙️

---

### 🧠 Why the title fits perfectly:

The title comes from a Persian poem praising the beauty of Kabul.

Example:

> Despite war and suffering, hope continues to survive.

👉 The title contrasts beauty and hope with the harsh realities faced by the characters.

---

### 🔗 Relevance to the Story:

The novel follows Mariam and Laila as they endure oppression, loss, and conflict.

Their courage and sacrifice become shining lights in difficult times.

👉 The title symbolizes the **hope, endurance, and human strength** at the heart of the novel.
