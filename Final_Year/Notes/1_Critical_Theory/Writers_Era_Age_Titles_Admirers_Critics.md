# 🟣 ARISTOTLE

### 📅 Era & Age

* Classical Greek Age
* **Classical Period (c. 500 BCE – 323 BCE)**
* 4th Century BCE
* Student of Plato

### 👤 Life & Death

* Born: **384 BCE**, Stagira, Greece
* Teacher of Alexander the Great
* Died: **322 BCE**

### 📚 Famous Works

* *Poetics*
* *Rhetoric*
* *Politics*
* *Nicomachean Ethics*

### 🏆 Titles

* **Father of Literary Criticism**
* **Father of Poetics**

### 👏 Admirers

* Philip Sidney
* John Dryden
* Neoclassical critics

### ⚖️ Critics

* Romantic critics thought his rules restricted imagination.

### 🌟 Contribution

* Developed theory of tragedy.
* Introduced Catharsis, Hamartia, Peripeteia.
* Established literary criticism as a discipline.
