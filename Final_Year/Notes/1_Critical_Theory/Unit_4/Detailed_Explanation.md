# Unit IV – Detailed Explanation

# 1. The Study of Poetry and The Function of Criticism at the Present Time – Matthew Arnold

## 📚 What are these essays about?

Matthew Arnold was one of the greatest critics of the Victorian Age.

His criticism focuses on:

* The importance of literature
* The role of criticism
* The value of poetry in society

Arnold believed literature could guide humanity in times of social and religious uncertainty.

---

# A. The Study of Poetry

## 🎯 Main Idea

Arnold argues that poetry is the highest form of literature and can replace religion and philosophy as a source of wisdom.

### Famous Statement

> "More and more mankind will discover that we have to turn to poetry to interpret life for us."

According to Arnold:

* Religion was losing influence.
* Science was challenging old beliefs.
* Poetry could provide moral and spiritual guidance.

---

## 📖 What is Poetry?

For Arnold, poetry is:

### "A Criticism of Life"

This is his most famous definition.

Poetry should:

* Interpret human life
* Present truth
* Offer moral insight

### Famous Quote

> "Poetry is at bottom a criticism of life."

---

## ⭐ Touchstone Method

Arnold introduced the famous:

### Touchstone Method

A "touchstone" is a standard used for testing quality.

Arnold suggests comparing passages from great poets to judge literary excellence.

Examples of great poets:

* Homer
* Dante Alighieri
* William Shakespeare
* John Milton

If a poem resembles these masterpieces, it is likely great literature.

---

## 🌟 Characteristics of Great Poetry

Arnold says great poetry must possess:

### High Seriousness

Meaning:

* Noble themes
* Profound truth
* Moral depth

Poetry should not merely entertain.

---

## 👤 Poet's Role

The poet should:

* Interpret life
* Reveal truth
* Guide humanity

---

## 🎭 Significance

### Literary Significance

* Elevated poetry's cultural importance.
* Introduced the Touchstone Method.
* Influenced modern criticism.

---

## 📝 Important Quotes

> "Poetry is at bottom a criticism of life."

> "The future of poetry is immense."

> "The best poetry will be found to have a power of forming, sustaining, and delighting us."

---

## 📌 Examination Point

Remember:

**The Study of Poetry = Poetry as Criticism of Life + Touchstone Method + High Seriousness**

---

# B. The Function of Criticism at the Present Time

## 🎯 Main Idea

Arnold explains the purpose of criticism.

He argues that criticism should be:

* Objective
* Intellectual
* Disinterested

Critics should seek truth rather than personal bias.

---

## 📖 What is Criticism?

Arnold defines criticism as:

### "A disinterested endeavor to learn and propagate the best that is known and thought in the world."

This is his most famous definition.

---

## ⭐ Role of the Critic

The critic should:

* Study ideas objectively
* Evaluate literature fairly
* Spread knowledge and culture

The critic must avoid:

* Political prejudice
* Religious prejudice
* Personal prejudice

---

## 🌍 Culture and Criticism

Arnold believed criticism promotes:

* Civilization
* Culture
* Intellectual growth

The critic helps society become more enlightened.

---

## 🌟 Significance

### Literary Significance

* Established criticism as an independent discipline.
* Emphasized objectivity in literary studies.

---

## 📝 Important Quotes

> "A disinterested endeavor to learn and propagate the best that is known and thought in the world."

> "The critic's task is to see the object as in itself it really is."

---

## 📌 Examination Point

Remember:

**Function of Criticism = Objective Criticism + Disinterestedness + Spread of Culture**

---

# 2. Tradition and the Individual Talent – T. S. Eliot

## 📚 What is it?

Published in 1919, this essay is one of the most influential works of modern literary criticism.

Eliot challenges the Romantic belief that poetry is merely personal expression.

Instead, he argues that great poetry emerges from a relationship between:

* Tradition
* Individual creativity

---

## 🎯 Main Idea

A great poet must:

* Know the literary tradition of the past.
* Add something new to it.

Thus:

### Tradition + Originality = Great Poetry

---

# 🌟 Meaning of Tradition

Eliot gives a new meaning to tradition.

Tradition does not mean blindly copying old writers.

It means:

* Understanding the entire literary heritage.
* Recognizing one's place within it.

A poet should know the works of earlier writers.

---

## 📖 Historical Sense

Eliot introduces the concept of:

### Historical Sense

This means awareness that:

* Past literature influences the present.
* Present literature also changes how we view the past.

### Famous Quote

> "The historical sense involves a perception of the pastness of the past and of its presence."

---

## 👤 Individual Talent

Although tradition is important, each poet contributes something unique.

The poet:

* Inherits tradition.
* Modifies tradition through new creations.

Thus literature continuously evolves.

---

# ⚗️ Theory of Impersonality

This is Eliot's most famous concept.

He argues:

### Poetry is not personal self-expression.

The poet should not simply pour personal emotions into poetry.

Instead, emotions should be transformed into art.

### Famous Quote

> "Poetry is not the turning loose of emotion, but an escape from emotion."

And:

> "Poetry is not the expression of personality, but an escape from personality."

---

## ⚗️ Catalyst Analogy

Eliot compares the poet to platinum in a chemical reaction.

The platinum:

* Helps create a reaction.
* Remains unchanged itself.

Similarly:

* The poet transforms emotions into poetry.
* Personal feelings are converted into artistic form.

The poet acts as a catalyst.

---

## 🌟 Significance

### Literary Significance

* Foundation of Modernist criticism.
* Challenged Romantic theories.
* Introduced the concepts of tradition and impersonality.

### Historical Significance

Influenced:

* New Criticism
* Modern literary studies
* Twentieth-century criticism

---

## 📝 Important Quotes

> "Poetry is not the expression of personality, but an escape from personality."

> "Poetry is not the turning loose of emotion, but an escape from emotion."

> "The historical sense involves a perception of the pastness of the past and of its presence."

---

## 📌 Examination Point

Remember:

**Tradition and the Individual Talent = Historical Sense + Tradition + Impersonality Theory**
