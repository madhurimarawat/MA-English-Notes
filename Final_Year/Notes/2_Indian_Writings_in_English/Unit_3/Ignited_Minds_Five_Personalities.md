## The five people and their main ideas

| Person                    | Main idea / quality                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1. Mahatma Gandhi**     | **Peace, non-violence and unity.** Gandhi represents the idea that people of different religions, races and languages can live together peacefully. He stresses love, truth and moral values.                         |
| **2. Albert Einstein**    | **Science, knowledge and human responsibility.** Einstein represents scientific thinking and intelligence. His ideas remind us that science should be used for the benefit of humanity, not for destruction.          |
| **3. Emperor Ashoka**     | **Transformation from violence to peace.** After the terrible **Kalinga War**, Ashoka changed from a conqueror into a compassionate ruler. He adopted **Ahimsa and Dharma** and spread the message of peace.          |
| **4. Abraham Lincoln**    | **Leadership, freedom and equality.** Lincoln represents courage and determination. He fought to preserve the United States and worked toward ending slavery, showing that a leader must stand for justice and unity. |
| **5. Caliph Omar (Umar)** | **Justice, simplicity and good governance.** He represents fairness, honesty and responsibility toward ordinary people. A ruler should serve people rather than seek personal power.                                  |

### 🌟 What Kalam wants us to learn

Kalam brings these five people together to ask a major question: **Why does humanity fight and destroy itself when human beings have the ability to create peace and prosperity?**

Their combined message is essentially:

**Peace + knowledge + compassion + justice + responsible leadership = a better humanity.**
