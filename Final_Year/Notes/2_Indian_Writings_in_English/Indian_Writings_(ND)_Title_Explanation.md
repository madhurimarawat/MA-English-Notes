# Unit – I

## 1. Indian Dancers – Sarojini Naidu

💃 **Indian Poetry**

### 📌 Meaning of the Title:

* **Indian** = Related to Indian culture and traditions 🇮🇳
* **Dancers** = Artists who express emotions through dance 💃

➡️ Means: *Indian dancers performing and expressing beauty through dance.*

---

### 🎯 Symbolism:

* Dancers symbolize **Indian cultural heritage** 🎭
* Dance symbolizes **joy, rhythm, and artistic expression** 🎶
* Their movements represent **grace and beauty** ✨

---

### 🧠 Why the title fits perfectly:

The poem vividly describes Indian dancers and the emotions reflected in their dance performances.

Example:

> The dancers move gracefully while expressing different moods and feelings.

👉 Title reflects the **central subject of the poem—Indian dance and culture.**

---

### 🔗 Relevance to the Story:

Every image in the poem focuses on the dancers' beauty, movement, and artistic skill.

👉 The title directly identifies the poem's **main theme and cultural focus.**
